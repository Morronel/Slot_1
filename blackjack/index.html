How to play

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their number, J Q K count 10, and an ace counts 11 or 1, whichever helps.

  • Blackjack (ace plus a ten-value card as your first two) pays 3 to 2.
  • A regular win pays 1 to 1. A tie is a push and your bet comes back.
  • Hit takes a card, Stand keeps your total.
  • Double doubles your bet on your first two cards and gives you exactly one more card.
  • Split a pair into two hands, each with its own bet, up to 4 hands. Split aces get one card each. Doubling after a split is allowed.
  • The dealer checks for blackjack when showing an ace or a ten, draws to 16 and stands on all 17s.
  • Six decks, reshuffled when the cut card comes out. No insurance.

With perfect play the return is about 99.4%. Every hand is random.
