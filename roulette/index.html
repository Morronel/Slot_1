    How to play

    Pick a chip, tap the table to place it, then spin. Tap a spot again to stack more. The ball lands on one of 37 numbers, 0 to 36.

    BetCoversPays
    Straight upOne number35 to 1
    Dozen1–12, 13–24 or 25–362 to 1
    Column12 numbers in a row of the table2 to 1
    Red or Black18 numbers1 to 1
    Odd or Even18 numbers1 to 1
    1–18 or 19–3618 numbers1 to 1

    Zero is green and loses every outside bet. Your stake comes back on top of the payout. Hover an outside bet to see the numbers it covers.

    Single zero: every bet returns about 97.3% over the long run. Turbo (the lightning button) makes the ball roll faster. Every spin is drawn with your browser's secure random generator before the ball leaves the hand, and each spin is independent.
